# Briefly - Product Specification

## 1. Core Purpose & Promise
"Turn messy client requests into clear project briefs."
Briefly is an AI project-intake and requirement-structuring tool designed for freelancers, agency owners, software developers, designers, project managers, and consultants.

## 2. Core Loop
Messy client communication (email, chat, voice transcript, notes, documents, screenshots) 
→ Structured project brief 
→ Highlights on ambiguous & missing items + contradictions 
→ AI-generated client clarification questions 
→ Actionable next steps & client-ready delivery

## 3. Key Principles
- Distinguish confirmed facts from inferences and assumptions.
- Preserve uncertainty with explicit ambiguity indicators.
- Source traceability: link every key requirement or ambiguity to source quotes.
- Contradiction detection: flag conflicting constraints (e.g. 5 pages vs 6 listed).
- Dual output: Internal Detailed Brief (with risks & unknowns) vs. Client-Ready Brief (professional, concise).
